SOURCE_MISSING
LNK-GT
Receiver figure of merit
G/T = G_rx - L_feed - 10 log10(T_sys) (dB/K), or given directly
SOURCE_MISSING

Carrier to noise density
C/N0 = EIRP - L_fs - L_atm - L_point - L_pol - L_impl + G/T - 10 log10(k) (dBHz)
SOURCE_MISSING

Link margin
