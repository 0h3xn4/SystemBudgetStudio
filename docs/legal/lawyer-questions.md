# Questions for a lawyer

Date: 2026-11-09. Written for a lawyer in the owner's country. The owner and Claude are not lawyers; nothing here states a legal conclusion. Facts are from the repository: [inventory.md](inventory.md), [legal-audit.md](legal-audit.md).

## Background in six lines

System Budget Studio is a personal, free, open-source, non-commercial desktop and command-line tool for early-design power, mass, thermal and link budgets of small satellites. It never uses the network. It is published on GitHub (24 releases, installers for Windows and Linux, currently under the MIT licence; the owner plans Apache-2.0). Most of the code and documentation were written by an AI system (Claude Code, Anthropic) under the owner's direction (312 of 440 commits have the AI as author; the owner reviewed and merged all). It bundles PySide6 and Qt (LGPL-3.0), fonts (OFL-1.1) and icons (ISC). It reads model files of IDM-CIC, a tool of CNES (the space agency of France), and shows invented data only.
