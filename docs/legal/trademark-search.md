# First search for the name "System Budget Studio"

Date: 2026-11-10. This is a first look, **not a trademark clearance** (lawyer question LQ-08). The official registers (EUIPO eSearch plus, the WIPO Global Brand Database, the USPTO trademark search, and the German DPMA register) are dynamic sites that could not be queried from the session. What was done instead: three general web searches (standard mode) for the exact name, the name with the subject words ("satellite power mass thermal link budget"), and "Budget Studio" with classes 9 and 42.

## Result

| Search | Found |
|---|---|
| `"System Budget Studio" trademark software` | No record for that name. Marks with "budget" in the name for software, on third-party aggregator sites: BUDGET 360 (class 9, live), BUDGETPAK (class 9, filed 2012), BUDGET PLUS (dead), CU/BUDGET-WARE (registered, software for financial institutions' budgets), BUDGETNOW, BUDGET U. |
| `"System Budget Studio" satellite power mass thermal link budget` | Nothing about a product of that name. Only general material on link budgets (for example a MathWorks tool) and papers. |
| `"Budget Studio"` with classes 9 and 42 | No record for "Budget Studio". Marks of a company called Budge Studios, Inc. (BUDGE, BUDGE GAMES, classes 9 and 42), a different name. |

## What this does not tell

- Aggregator pages are not the registers; their status fields may be wrong or old.
- "Budget" is a common word; the marks above show that software marks with it exist. A mark may also be refused or limited because the words are descriptive for a budget tool.
- Variants, translations (German) and national registers (DPMA) were not searched.
- The name of the owner's other tools ("SpaceMissionStudio", "Harness Design Studio" and so on) was not searched.
